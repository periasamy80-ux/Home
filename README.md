Library Highlights
📚 Rich Learning Resources

Textbooks, reference books, general reading materials, competitive examination books, journals, newspapers, and other academic resources.

Library services designed to support UG students in their coursework, examinations, projects, higher studies, and career preparation.

A dedicated collection of books and study materials to help students prepare for government and other competitive examinations.

💻 Digital Learning

Students are encouraged to make effective use of free digital resources, e-books, e-journals, online courses, and educational websites.

Selected UG student projects and academic publications are preserved as useful reference resources.

New books, newspapers, journals, important academic information, and library activities are regularly brought to the attention of users.

The Library strives to provide accessible learning opportunities for students from different educational and socio-economic backgrounds.
