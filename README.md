About the Library
Government Arts & Science College Library

The Government Arts & Science College Library, Thennangur is an important academic support centre of the college. It serves students, teaching faculty, and other members of the college community by providing access to books, reference materials, journals, magazines, digital resources, and other learning materials.

The Library supports the teaching, learning, research, and overall academic development of the college. It encourages students to develop regular reading habits, improve their knowledge, and make effective use of information resources.

Our Vision

To develop a student-centred library that provides easy access to quality information resources and supports academic excellence, lifelong learning, and knowledge development.

Our Mission
To provide the right information to the right user at the right time.
To support the teaching and learning activities of the college.
To promote reading and self-learning habits among students.
To provide access to print and digital information resources.
To support students in their academic assignments, examinations, projects, and competitive examinations.
To introduce students to modern information and digital resources.
Library Services

The Library provides services such as:

Book lending and return
Reference service
Current awareness service
New book display
Newspaper and magazine service
Competitive examination resources
Previous question papers
Student project and academic resources
Digital and e-resources
Library orientation and user guidance
Our Users

The Library primarily serves:

Undergraduate students
Postgraduate students
Teaching faculty
Non-teaching staff
Researchers and other authorized users
Our Goal

Our goal is to create a welcoming, accessible, and technology-supported learning environment where every student can discover information, develop knowledge, improve academic performance, and become a confident lifelong learner.
